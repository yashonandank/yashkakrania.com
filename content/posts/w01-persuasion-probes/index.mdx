## The question

Replies that earn a Δ on CMV changed someone's mind. If persuasiveness is *linearly represented*, a simple probe on the residual stream should separate Δ from non-Δ replies, and adding that direction back should make generations more persuasive.

## What I read

The three sources in the frontmatter show up at the bottom of this post **and** on the Library page automatically.

## Key ideas, in my words

A steering vector is the difference between mean activations of the two groups at layer $\ell$:

$$
v_\ell = \mu_\ell(\Delta) - \mu_\ell(\neg\Delta), \qquad h' = h + \alpha \, v_\ell
$$
