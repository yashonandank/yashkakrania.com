r/ChangeMyView is a rare natural dataset for persuasion: when a reply changes the original poster's mind, they award it a **Δ (delta)**. That gives labelled examples of text that *worked* versus text that didn't, on the same topic.

The plan: extract residual-stream activations for delta and non-delta replies, train linear probes layer by layer, then use the difference of means as a steering vector and see what happens to generations.
